# BabyFoot player visual asset

English names throughout. Red and blue variants share one low-poly mesh in the source scene. Four material slots; team colour changes on slot 0.

Scale is metres. Height 0.141 m is provisional. Origin is the provisional rod centre, with rod rotation along local X in Blender and front along -Y. FBX uses -Z forward and Y up. Validate the imported orientation and foot height before replacing the Unity prototype.

No shaft hole is modeled because the shaft placement has not been measured. No collider or rigidbody is exported. Use simple gameplay colliders in Unity. Facial details and clothing are an approximation of the photo reference, not an exact replica.

The arena and the presentation camera/lights are excluded from the FBX exports. The editable blend contains only the player studio scene.
